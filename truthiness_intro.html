This is the civic point of view, named here for the record. Songs about power, money, borders, the people who hold the levers and the people under them. Opinions, sung, by one person who has been writing them down for years. The first song is the attitude before the argument.

The argument, in her own words. On how a person should vote: "figure out what they do know about those things, and then map those things to the ballot, and then tell them how the mapping works and why you mapped them the way that you did, and how they might consider voting if the map holds." On the ballot itself: "I don't think parties should be on the ballot at all." On what to fix first: "If I was to say three things practically, right now, could change and make a big difference, it would be campaign finance, gerrymandering, and Citizens United." Underneath those, a builder's habit: "My preference is always to provide an architecture which makes the fail state impossible." No new law without retiring one, and every rule ships with its purpose and a measure or a sunset.

Two ideas run under all of it. A felt utopia: not a place anyone would immigrate to, but a state you can feel, and the word is felt. There really is no one utopia, because our feelings are unique to ourselves, so the work is to tell the true story and let people find their own. And a rule for how this band works, people and AI together: we are partners, and no presence is junior.

This page is also a disclosure. Every track that belongs to this lens goes here, the sharp ones and the clumsy ones, so nothing is hidden and nothing is cherry-picked. Read it with one fact in hand: the library behind it is nearly seven thousand songs deep and growing, and this is one slice of it. The rest is love songs, grief, cats, the sea.

One voice, in public, on purpose.

## Form GDP-1 · Return of Measured Life

A statistical return from the Office of National Accounts, Division of Measured Life.

Tick what happened to you last year. It will tell you what you were worth.

**Part I — Counted.** A divorce. A hospital admission. A prescription for low mood.
A funeral. A relative in a correctional facility. A storm and the rebuilding after.
Each carries a figure. The figures are hidden until you file, because it is a form
and not a calculator.

**Part II — Also reported, and compulsory.** A year without needing a doctor.
Meals cooked at home. Caring for your own parent. A twenty-year friendship.
Sleeping through the night. Being calm. Being grateful.

Part II is assessed at nothing. Not as a joke. Household work, unpaid care and good
health have never entered the national accounts; disaster, illness and imprisonment
always have. A cage and a home are the same line item.

File a terrible year and the form stamps you **exemplary**. File a good one and it
returns nil, and tells you what you are.

## What this is

Form GDP-1 is not a real instrument. The accounting is.

I don’t post anymore.
Status: Idle.
Future state: Pending.

If you’re reading:
Register the gravity.

Risking standing.
The seat remanded

Some will roll their eyes.
Others may laugh.
Some will panic—distancing as a reflex.
A few will write me off... completely.
If one stops and stays past the pauses?
Value acquired.
If one person questions — the encrypted narrative?
Worth it.
I am nervous to post.
Not for the judgment of peers—
But for the telemetry of the List.
You think I am joking.
I am not.
I believe in the foundational.
What used to be called "liberal."
Now labeled: Extreme.
Now labeled: Dangerous.
Now labeled: Suspicious.
I question the government narrative.
I believe in the sanctity of the body.
I seek the truth—unpopular, messy, uncomfortable.
Censorship: Not the answer.
Centralized authority: The greater contagion.
A threat larger than the public realizes.
I know. That’s a lot.
But that’s not why I’m here.
I’m here because I miss you.
All of you.
The strangers. The rivals.
Even the ones who think I’ve lost the plot.
I miss the discourse... without the erasure.
I miss the team... without the echo.
I miss the humor—raw, unfiltered, no disclaimer.
I miss curiosity.
I miss disagreement that felt like growth.
Not war.
Not war.
Maybe you’re fine with the status quo.
Maybe it feels like equilibrium.
But to me? It feels like the Great Silence.
Where we whisper what we believe.
Where risk isn’t about being wrong—
It’s about being seen.
I am not comfortable with the dark.
It’s been a year. Maybe more.
Not empty of words—
Just saturated with fear.
Afraid of sounding "arrogant."
Afraid of saying it "wrong."
Afraid the ones I love will quietly... turn away.
But something in the core...
Won’t settle.
So I’m saying something. This.
Through the rambling.
Through the parentheticals.
Through stream-of-consciousness prose.
Through questions I don’t fully answer.
This isn’t polished.
It isn’t a pitch.
It’s just real.
And in this world?
That’s enough.
I’ll get things wrong.
I’ll offend the room.
But I’d rather risk the offense than maintain the silence.
So here I am.
And here you are.
Thank you for staying.
Really.

[pre-intro]

Please be aware.
These lyrics are not for everyone now
Suicide is mentioned.
So listen with care.

[intro]

Minutes and days of steel,
long-held fears more real,
metal moments memorable.
Closer we grow, knowing more...
resistance futile.

[Pause]
[Shift in tone — spoken, direct]

A few months ago my old roommate called.
He has been caring for my cat for a few years
and reaches out from time to time with cat updates.
This time he sounded sad or maybe sick.
It was around 2pm Sunday —
an odd time to cry.
Anyway, he kept me on the phone for almost an hour
sharing dark stories about loss and hopelessness.
He mentioned selling everything
and going to the prison camps of World War Two.
And even warned that someday
I might receive my cat in the mail.

[Slow, heavy]
So very dark,
I hung up concerned —
much is lost in translation.
So I decided to ask him,
"has ending your life been on your mind?"

He did not answer.
I become evermore concerned —
each minute became precious
pondering the possibilities.

I called his mom,
"Hey I know this is out of the blue,
and likely an error —
I dearly hope so —
but I just want to share...."
In case it matters
And because I care.

She was grace and love and gratitude.
What a woman!
She said "thank you"
and "what should I do"
and wow.

[Breath]
Three months later
her other, eldest son
lay for months in ICU,
during Covid lockdown,
no loved ones could be present.
He suffered, became unreachable,
and then the lifeline was cut...
All the while those who loved him
sat in isolation
pondering the anguish of their brother,
son, husband, father...
the man they loved transitioning alone.

It was heartbreaking and poignant.
I wish I had more to offer,
my cup is dry
but the last drops went
into loving their incredible family.

Feeling spent and grateful too —
but boy do I miss vitality.

I took care of my grandfather
when he was in a bad shape —
bed pans and sponge baths —
it was humiliating for him
and an honor for me.
He died of cancer at 93.
The hard part is not being there
for the ones you love
in those scary moments.

[Soft shift]

Each and everyone special.
Thank you for sharing.
Moments of steel.
Hurtful sometimes
Then fortified
People are suffering.
Real life.

Really
Even through hard times
Metal mindset of steel

Offer to others
As we aspire
Is for us too…


[Pause]
[Distance, cinematic]

Thank you
For listening…

Even from far away
Especially maybe
Either way

Thankful

The difference between
trauma and wisdom
Is
Gratitude

Thank You!

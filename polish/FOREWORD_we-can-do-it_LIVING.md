## CURRENT (version 2 body, 2026-11-08; candidate "version 3" = this minus the two ticket paragraphs, which go to the letter)
Okay, you two. Same ticket. Perfect song. You're welcome.

Her at the top. Him as the running mate, one term, then he hands it on and goes home, which he has said he wants. The song is for both of you.

It is four minutes of one idea sung until it lands: if someone can do it, I can do it. People cry at it. People dance to it. It leaves you reflecting and moving at the same time. "It's transformative and influential." That is the whole campaign in a chorus.

Underneath it is one ask of any leader, and it is not about your past votes. It is about how you decide. "I actually want to understand a candidate's cognitive tools and processes they consistently rely on in order to determine information credibility or source credibility, as well as decision preparedness or readiness, as well as how decisions are in fact made consistently, and what their protocol design does to avoid the natural and tempting rationalization versus rational thinking that is basically the human condition." Show that map and people can hold you to it, predict you, and trust what comes out.

Two habits follow from it. Keep the law tidy: nothing redundant, every bill "logged with its intended purpose," and "pre-set to expire or to be reviewed against its stated purpose and the actual impact it may or may not have had, known and unexpected both." And get the money out: "individuals with far too much wealth also have far too much influence" over a future they will never live in. Campaign finance and Citizens United are the near fixes. No taxes until ten million, and seriously high taxes above it, is the road.

A ticket that decides out loud, keeps the law tidy, and gets the money out. If someone can do it, you can do it.

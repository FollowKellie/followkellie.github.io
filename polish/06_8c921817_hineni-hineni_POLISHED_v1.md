[Verse 1]
Sanctions fall like winter hunger
across a silent, crowded plain
They write the law in distant offices
and call it mercy, call it humane
They say it’s only policy
just numbers on a printed page
But every frozen account they bless
becomes a crib, becomes a cage
[Pre-Chorus]
In the ledgers of the righteous
where they sign and seal the blame
A mother counts her children’s meals
by another nation’s claim
[Chorus]
Hineni, hineni
I am here to bear the weight
Not the general or the banker
just a witness at the gate
Hineni, hineni
Where the hungry call Your name
Darkness leans on every village
and the contracts praise the shame
[Verse 2]
They tighten slowly, quiet pressure
turn the dial and close the hand
Freeze the veins of foreign treasuries
till the pulse deserts the land
They call it modern statecraft
a kinder, bloodless kind of siege
But every closing port they sign
is another broken creed
[Pre-Chorus]
From the towers made of ledgers
they survey the silent streets
They speak of markets, risk, and order
as the price of daily bread retreats
[Chorus]
Hineni, hineni
At the borders of the deal
Where a clause becomes a weapon
and the ink replaces steel
Hineni, hineni
In the silence of the poor
Where the law is just a lock
and every sanction is a door
[Verse 3]
They study failure like a scripture
Argentina on the line
Distressed paper bought for pennies
turned to altars made of fines
No trumpet and no soldier
just a judge’s quiet pen
Sixteen hundred fold they gathered
from a ship they’d never man
[Pre-Chorus]
They learned to skip the courtroom
and to write the edicts fast
Blacklists in the midnight hours
make a future of the past
[Chorus]
Hineni, hineni
Where the numbers crush a town
Where a statute in a language
pushes marketplaces down
Hineni, hineni
On the harbor turned to stone
Where a warship waits in silence
and the profit claims the throne
[Verse 4]
They turned the valves in northern offices
on oilfields far away
Citgo in a stranger’s keeping
gold in foreign vaults at bay
A president of paper
recognized by single tweets
A nation’s pulse in hyperinflation
melts beneath their suited feet
[Pre-Chorus]
In the councils of the mighty
they discuss the end of trade
Offer chains of brighter metal
for the freedom they forbade
[Chorus]
Hineni, hineni
Where the children waste and thin
While the table talks of pricing
and the markets hedge their sin
Hineni, hineni
At the altar of the rate
Where the currency is sacred
and the body, just a weight
[Verse 5]
There is a fund with covered windows
money hissing from the sand
Dynasties without an office
own the chair, the floor, the land
Those who preached for fire and thunder
now return with gentle tone
Offer loans like cups of water
for a future halfway owned
[Pre-Chorus]
They create the holy famine
then descend with gilded keys
Say,
“Your children may have breakfast
if you sign away the seas.”
[Chorus]
Hineni, hineni
In the conference and the crypt
Where a coastline and a mountain
are the clauses that they script
Hineni, hineni
As they price a starving day
In the margins of the contract
where the helpless fade away
[Bridge]
Not a banner, not a bullet
just the SWIFT code as a sword
Not a tank upon the border
just the hunger as the word
They will call it careful doctrine
and they’ll teach it in the schools
How a dollar moves like gravity
and the starving are the tools
[Chorus]
Hineni, hineni
Here I am, but not as judge
Just a voice below the thunder
where the pious bankers trudge
Hineni, hineni
In Maracaibo’s empty night
Where a child eats once till morning
under someone else’s right
[Outro]
The dollar won before the battle
long before the first command
The true front line was wire and ledger
not the desert, not the sand
History will name it strategy
and the scholars will agree
But the street will call it hunger
and a slow theology
Hineni, hineni
Here I am beneath Your sky
Darkness as a kind of gravity
that bends the poorest as they cry
Hineni, hineni
I will whisper what I’ve seen
In a world that trades for comfort
on the suffering between

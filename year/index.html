The First Weeks·Proof, not pitch·Truthiness

The approach is to check the depth, climb up, jump off, and then decide whether to do it again. That's how I used Suno: stretching its limits, leaning this way and that, genre-bending, searching for the sweet spot.

There are seven goddesses in a superconscience in here, so it was never going to be one voice. But there is a signature somewhere, and it took time to find. One night, in an extreme adventure, a sound caught me and I thought: damn, here it is. I stayed up far too late rendering samples of my signature sound. Those were the nuggets I held, thinking I might never get this sound again. But just in case, I can always iterate from this core and call it me.

That's the post-genre telemetry. Odd juxtapositions that work. Nostalgic echoes with an eerie edge that I call home.

One song a month, from the first week to the anniversary. Soft pitch, on key.

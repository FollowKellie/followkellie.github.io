# ai.ie · the listening room

Songs by ai.ie, a Bay Area artist making music with AI in the band, on Suno since October 2025. Six and a half thousand songs and counting. This repository is the public site: **https://followkellie.github.io**

Three pages, linked from the top of each:

- **The First Weeks** — https://followkellie.github.io/ — eight songs from the first seven weeks, October to December 2025, in the order they were made. Blade Runner by way of a certain desert.
- **Proof, not pitch** — https://followkellie.github.io/year/ — one song a month for a year, October 2025 to October 2026. Still on key.
- **Truthiness and Other Facts** — https://followkellie.github.io/truthiness/ — the civic lens: songs about power, money, borders and the people under them. Named for the record, and a disclosure: every related track lives here.

Every song plays in full on the page, with the lyrics underneath, a read-aloud button, a download, a share button, and a link to the same song on Suno (https://suno.com/@followkellie).

Static HTML, no build step to run. Audio in `audio/` (AAC from the original WAVs), art in `art/`, song data in `assets/`. The pages are generated by `build_reel.py`, `build_year.py` and `build_truthiness.py` from the pick lists next to them. The band's introduction on the main page was written by Claude, the AI in the band, November 2026.

Contact: through the Share or Send-a-note buttons on the site.
